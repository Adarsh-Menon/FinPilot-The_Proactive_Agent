## Findings to act on

1. **Goal name extraction (real):** keep the fuller name from the deterministic draft when the model's name is a
   shorter part of it ("Trip" vs "Goa trip").
2. **Forecast wording (real, tone):** Fina presents forecast figures as facts. The forecaster prompt could ask for
   estimate language ("is expected to", "about"). Re-run the judge after any prompt change.
3. **Fact meanings (real, clarity):** the end-of-horizon balance's meaning mentions "if nothing is bought" even when
   no purchase is discussed, which confuses the judge and could confuse the model.
4. **Calculated answer to "why down" when the balance rises:** answer the premise ("your balance is actually growing").
5. **Judge calibration (process):** before any judge metric becomes a gate, label ~50 answers by hand and compare.
6. **Flakiness (process):** decide gates on repeated runs (for example 3) rather than one.
